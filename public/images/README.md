# Replacing store images

The included WebP images are editable starter photography. Replace any of them with your own
dress photos and update the matching path in:

- Hero images: `src/data/heroSlides.ts`
- Product images: `src/data/products.ts`
- Category images: `src/data/categories.ts`
- About image: `src/pages/About.tsx`

Recommended formats are WebP or optimized JPG. Product photos look best in a 3:4 portrait ratio. Hero photos look best in a 4:5 portrait ratio.
